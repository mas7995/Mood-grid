# The Mood Grid

A simple daily mood tracker, inspired by **Step 4: The Mood Grid**.

> Every evening, check in with yourself. How did you feel by the end of the day?
> Did the routine make a difference? Use the Mood Grid to track your emotions and
> see your growth over time.

## How to use

1. Open `index.html` in any web browser (no installation or build step needed).
2. Pick the mood that best represents your day from the palette.
3. Click the circle for the day to "color it in."
4. Click **Log Today** for a one-tap entry for the current day.

Click a day again with the same mood selected to clear it. You can only log
today or past days — future days are locked.

## The six moods

| Color        | Mood          |
| ------------ | ------------- |
| 🟢 Green       | Happy         |
| 🟩 Light Green | Content       |
| 🔵 Light Blue  | Neutral       |
| 🟣 Purple      | Sad           |
| 🟠 Orange      | Angry         |
| 🔴 Red         | Overwhelmed   |

## Features

- A full year of mood circles, one card per month.
- Navigate between years with the arrows.
- A year summary showing how your moods break down.
- **Export / Import** your data as JSON to back it up or move between devices.

## Where is my data stored?

Everything is saved in your browser's `localStorage` on the device you use.
Nothing is sent anywhere. Use **Export** to keep a backup.
